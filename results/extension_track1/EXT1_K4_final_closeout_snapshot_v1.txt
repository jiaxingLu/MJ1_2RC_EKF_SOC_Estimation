6. Mechanistic interpretation
----------------------------
The evidence supports the following bounded mechanism:

a) shortening posterior memory repairs the cumulative-memory failure at the
   aggregate Case-C level but increases responsiveness to local shadow-model
   identification targets.

b) shadow-model alpha targets and EKF-useful conditional alpha targets are
   not identical; this target mismatch is an architecture-level property and
   is not unique to Case C.

c) under high-amplitude Case C, sustained high-SOC regression appears because
   realized correction energy can exceed the residual-cancellation benefit.
   Exact per-bin residual-correction geometry supports this statement.

55-60% SOC:
  Frozen->K4 total SSE change = +67.405840 %
  Frozen->K4-state alpha=1 SSE change = +91.698966 %
  Interpretation: state-path degradation is material in this region.

  alpha=1-state->K4 alpha-layer SSE change = +186.297048 %

  over-corrects strongly and reverses the benefit.

65-70% SOC:
