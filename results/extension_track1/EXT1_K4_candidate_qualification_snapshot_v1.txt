Sensor-bias evidence
--------------------
The original predeclared K=4 sensor-bias stress remains formally FAIL
because EXT1_A at +/-20 mA produced update-count shifts of -2 against
the predeclared |Delta N| <= 1 criterion. This result is not rewritten.

Matched attribution subsequently showed:
- the same update-count shifts occur in frozen v1.0 on the same A/B datasets;
- K=4 materially amplifies internal final-alpha current-offset sensitivity;
- the dominant mechanism is current-offset-induced shadow-SOC drift feeding
  the nominal R0(SOC) LUT index;
- protecting the R0-LUT SOC index in oracle diagnostics removes about 92-93%
  of K=4 current-bias final-alpha sensitivity on A/B;
- voltage-offset sensitivity of alpha remains numerically negligible.
